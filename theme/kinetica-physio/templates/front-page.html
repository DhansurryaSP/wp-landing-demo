FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. If you have scans or a surgeon's protocol, bring them to your first visit.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes that let us see and move the area being treated, such as shorts for knee problems.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. You get an estimate in writing after the first assessment, and we review it as you progress.

Can I claim this on insurance?

Many policies cover physiotherapy. We provide itemised receipts; check your policy for the exact cover.
